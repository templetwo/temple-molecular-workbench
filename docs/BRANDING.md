# Temple Lab identity

The identity pairs a graphite studio surface with a bright lime atom mark. Three crossing orbits express exploration at different scales; the nucleus anchors the composition.

- [Vector app mark](../public/temple-lab-mark.svg) — resolution-independent, square icon.
- [Vector wordmark](../public/temple-lab-logo.svg) — horizontal identity with the Molecular Workbench subtitle.
- [PNG app mark](../public/temple-lab-mark.png) — 1024 × 1024 pixels.
- [PNG wordmark](../public/temple-lab-logo.png) — 2400 × 560 pixels.
- The macOS bundle uses a native multi-resolution `.icns` version of the app mark.

Primary colors: lime `#d5f582`, graphite `#151d17`, pale text `#edf3e7`. Use the mark on a clear surface, keep its proportions, and leave space around it. The logo artwork is included under the project's MIT license.
